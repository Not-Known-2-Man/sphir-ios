# Sphir Support

Welcome to Sphir — your calm space to capture and collect what matters.
Everything you save stays private, on your device.

## Quick start
1. **Add a card.** Tap **+ Card**, choose a type, then pick a source:
   - **Paste URL** — paste a link; Sphir fills in the title, description, and
     image automatically.
   - **From Screenshots or Photos** — pick an image; Sphir reads any text in
     it using on-device recognition.
   - **Take Photo** — capture something with the camera.
2. **Pick a collection** to file the card into, then tap **Save**.
3. **Make collections.** Tap **+ Collection**, name it, choose a privacy
   label, and Continue.
4. **Find things.** Use the search bar to filter collections and cards by
   title, text, or tags.
5. **Edit or remove.** Long-press any card to Edit or Delete it.

## Saving from other apps (Share Extension)
You can send links and text to Sphir straight from Safari, Mail, or Messages:
1. Tap the **Share** button in the other app.
2. Choose **Sphir** from the share sheet. (First time: tap **More**, then
   enable Sphir if you don't see it.)
3. Confirm the collection and tap **Save**. Tap **View** to jump to the saved
   card.

New cards appear in Sphir the next time you open it.

## FAQ

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely on your device. Sphir has no accounts and no servers — nothing is
uploaded. Deleting the app removes your data.

**Does Sphir need an account or internet?**
No account, ever. Sphir works offline. It only goes online to fetch a preview
(title, description, image) for a link you add.

**How does text extraction (OCR) work?**
When you import a screenshot or photo, Sphir recognizes the text on your
device using Apple's Vision framework — English and Spanish are supported.
The text becomes editable and searchable.

**I don't see Sphir in the share sheet.**
Tap **More** (or **Edit**) at the end of the share sheet and turn Sphir on.
You may need to scroll the row of app icons.

**A link didn't fetch a preview.**
Some sites block automated requests or don't provide preview info. You can
still add a title and description by hand and save the card.

**Are my collections private?**
Yes. Privacy labels are for your own organization, and your library never
leaves your device.

**How do I delete a card or collection?**
Long-press a card to Edit or Delete it (Delete asks for confirmation).
